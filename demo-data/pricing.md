# Larkspur Notes: pricing

All prices are in USD. Prices below are the only prices that may be quoted by email.

| Plan       | Monthly price | Included call hours | Overage per hour | Seats     |
| ---------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- | --------- |
| Starter    | $49           | 20                  | $3.00            | 3         |
| Team       | $199          | 100                 | $2.50            | 15        |
| Scale      | $799          | 500                 | $2.00            | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Custom        | Custom              | Custom           | Unlimited |

## What each plan includes

- **Starter**: REST API, webhooks, TypeScript and Python SDKs, email support.
- **Team**: everything in Starter, plus HubSpot and Salesforce sync, meeting bots, and
  support with a one business day response time.
- **Scale**: everything in Team, plus configurable data retention, SSO (SAML), a shared
  Slack channel and a 99.9% uptime SLA.
- **Enterprise**: everything in Scale, plus a custom contract, security review support and
  a dedicated solutions engineer. Enterprise starts at 2,000 call hours per month.

## Billing rules

- Annual billing gives two months free (pay for 10 months, get 12).
- There is a 14-day free trial on Starter and Team with 5 call hours included.
- Startups under two years old with fewer than 20 employees get 25% off Team for the
  first year. They must ask for it.
- Non-profits and education get 30% off any self-serve plan.
- No other discounts may be offered by email. Requests for custom pricing, volume
  discounts or Enterprise terms must go to a call with Maya.
